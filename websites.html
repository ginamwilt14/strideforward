Common questions

Questions about
your website.

Short answers about prices, payment, time, tools, and ownership. If yours is not here, ask on the call.

How much does a website cost?

Essentials is $695 and Complete is $1,795. You can pay in full, in three parts, or with the 12-month plan ($137 or $209 a month, with a year of care included). Care is $79 a month and Grow is $199 a month after launch.

How does the 12-month plan work?

You pay the first two months to start, and the other ten are charged automatically each month. Your site launches and care starts at launch. After the twelfth payment, we hand the site over to you or keep it running on care. You can pay off the rest of the build at any time.

What happens if a plan payment is missed?

We send a friendly reminder first. At 30 days late, care pauses. At 60 days late, the site goes offline until the plan is caught up. Your domain stays yours the whole time.

Can I buy without a call?

Yes. Buy online and we start with a short questionnaire and a kickoff call within two business days. If it isn't a fit, you get a full refund before any work starts. If you'd rather talk first, book a discovery call.

How long does a build take?

About two weeks for Essentials and four to five weeks for Complete, from kickoff to launch, depending on how quickly reviews come back.

Do you build on Wix, Squarespace, or WordPress?

No. We build every site with Claude, GitHub, and Netlify. That keeps the pages fast and light, and when you take the site over, every file is yours.

Who owns the site and the domain?

Your domain is yours from day one, bought in your own name, along with your Google Business Profile, Search Console, form messages, words, and photos. The site runs from our GitHub and Netlify during the build and while you're on care, Grow, or the 12-month plan. Once the build is paid, we hand it over to your own accounts whenever you ask.

What do I need to have ready?

A domain in your name, any photos you want to use, and time to review each page. If you don't know an answer to something yet, that's completely okay. We talk it through.

Can my site connect to my email list and follow-up?

Yes. Contact and sign-up forms can feed your email list. If we also set up AI workflows with you, a new inquiry can start a drafted reply that a person reviews before it goes out.
