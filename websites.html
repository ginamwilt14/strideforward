How a build works

From a conversation
to a site you love.

Most builds take three to six weeks from the signed scope note to launch, depending on the number of pages and how quickly reviews come back.

  1. 01

    Start with a conversation.

    A free discovery call, then a friendly questionnaire about you, your people, and the sites you love. Skip anything that doesn't fit yet, and we talk it through.

  2. 02

    A scope note before anything is built.

    Within two business days you get one page with the pages, who does what, the timeline, and the fee. Nothing gets built until you say yes.

  3. 03

    A private preview, then launch.

    You click through every page on your phone and laptop before it goes live, and we change what doesn't feel like you.

After launch

Take it with you,
or let us keep it running.

Your site launches from our GitHub and Netlify accounts. At launch you choose where it lives next. Either way, the site and the domain are yours.

Option 1 · Hand it over

The site moves into your own accounts.

What happens
We move the site into your own GitHub and Netlify accounts and give you written instructions for small changes.
Good for
Owners who like to make their own edits, or who have someone who can.

Option 2 · Monthly site care

We keep it running and up to date.

What happens
The site stays on our hosting. Each month we check that the forms and the domain work and make a set number of updates. Bigger changes, like a new page, get their own short scope note.
Good for
Owners who would rather hand the keeping-up to someone else.

Common questions

Questions about
your website.

Short answers about cost, time, tools, and ownership. If yours is not here, ask on the call.

How much does a website cost?

Every site is costed in a one-page scope note after a free discovery call, so the fee matches the pages and features you need. You see the fee before you commit, and nothing gets built until you say yes.

How long does a build take?

Most builds take three to six weeks from the signed scope note to launch, depending on the number of pages and how quickly reviews come back.

Do you build on Wix, Squarespace, or WordPress?

No. We build every site with Claude, GitHub, and Netlify. That keeps the pages fast and light, and when you take the site over, every file is yours.

Who owns the site and the domain?

You do. You buy the domain in your own name. At launch you choose: the site moves into your own GitHub and Netlify accounts with written instructions, or it stays with us on monthly site care.

What do I need to have ready?

A domain in your name, any photos you want to use, and time to review each page. If you don't know an answer to something yet, that's completely okay. We talk it through.

Can my site connect to my email list and follow-up?

Yes. Contact and sign-up forms can feed your email list. If we also set up AI workflows with you, a new inquiry can start a drafted reply that a person reviews before it goes out.
