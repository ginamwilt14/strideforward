Topics

Where the learning
goes deep.

For teachers, guides, paraprofessionals, instructional coaches, and school leaders. We adapt the focus and depth to your team’s starting point.

Inclusive practices

Practical moves that make every classroom more accessible — from language and grouping to feedback and belonging cues. Educators leave with concrete routines they can adapt without overhauling their curriculum.

Co-teaching

Beyond "you take reading, I'll take math." Roles, planning cadence, shared assessment, and the conversations that keep co-teaching pairs — general and special education, teacher and paraprofessional — working well over a full year.

Self-directed learning

Structures and scaffolds that grow student agency — goal-setting, choice architecture, conferencing routines, and the classroom systems that make self-direction sustainable rather than chaotic. For teachers, guides, and Montessori-adjacent models.

UDL and differentiation

Universal Design for Learning as a planning lens, paired with practical differentiation moves that don't require three versions of every worksheet. How to design once for variability and adjust in the moment based on what learners are showing.

Teaching durable skills

Communication, collaboration, self-management, problem-solving, and other skills employers and life ask for. How to teach them explicitly inside content instruction — with observable indicators, common language across grade levels, and assessment approaches that hold up.

Custom-designed to your outcomes

None of these are off-the-shelf decks. We shape scope, sequence, and delivery around the outcomes you name — the practice you want to see in classrooms, the priorities driving your school-improvement plan, and the constraints of your calendar and staffing.

Learning design

Outcomes first.
Then the learning experience.

  1. 01 / DEFINE

    Name the shift.

    Identify a specific practice, understand the current context, and agree on what useful progress would look like.

  2. 02 / PRACTICE

    Make time to try it.

    Use modeling, rehearsal, reflection, and work with real classroom materials. Leave with something ready to use.

  3. 03 / SUSTAIN

    Support the next step.

    Connect the session to coaching, team routines, and opportunities to reflect on what happens in practice.

Coaching & induction

Support beyond
the session.

Build on facilitated learning with support for the people putting it into practice.

Instructional coaching

Coaching cycles built around observable practice moves — pre-conference, classroom observation, evidence-based debrief, and a next-step commitment. Structured to shift practice measurably over a semester, not one drop-in visit.

New teacher induction

Induction support for first- and second-year teachers and alternative-certification candidates — mentoring, observation and feedback, video analysis in learning communities, and the conversations new teachers need in year one.

Inclusive & co-teaching support

Coaching for special and general education pairs on the planning cadence, roles, shared assessment, and belonging cues that make co-teaching work — grounded in classroom and IEP experience, not theory.
