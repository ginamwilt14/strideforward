How we build

Your accounts.
Your voice and rules.

Everything is set up so it keeps working for you, whoever runs it next.

  • Built in your accounts. AI helpers, social media, and email live in accounts in your name, and you pay your own subscriptions. Websites launch from ours, then move to yours at handover or stay with us on monthly site care. We never ask for your passwords or card numbers.
  • Drafts, then a person. Helpers draft, sort, and remind. A person sends, posts, pays, and decides.
  • Your voice and rules written in. Each helper follows your voice and a short list of things it never does.
  • Your data stays careful. We check your data settings together at setup. Customer personal details and records covered by privacy rules (student, health, or donor financial records) stay out unless your own policy allows them.
  • Results we measure with you. We don't promise hours saved or revenue up front. We track what changes, together.

Common questions

Questions about
the packages.

Short answers about cost, tools, and what happens after the build. If yours is not here, ask on the call.

How much does a package cost?

Every package is costed for your project in a one-page scope note after a free discovery call. You see the work, the timeline, and the fee before you commit, and nothing starts until you say yes.

Do I need to know anything about AI?

No. We set everything up in your own accounts, write the instructions in plain language, and train you on your own setup. You also get a written guide to what each helper does and what it never does.

Which tools do you use?

Claude for AI helpers, GitHub and Netlify for websites, Buffer for scheduling social posts, and MailerLite for email. You own the accounts and pay your own subscriptions, so what we build keeps working if you stop working with us.

Can you keep it running after it's built?

Yes. With build and manage, we keep your site, posts, email, and helpers running each month, and you approve what goes out. Set hours and a monthly fee go in your scope note and are reviewed every quarter.

Can I learn to build it myself instead?

Yes. NextReady Studio, a sister brand, teaches owners to build and run their own AI helpers through coaching and workshops. Many people start with a package and learn to build the next one themselves.

Is my customer information safe?

We check your data settings together at setup, and every helper has rules about what it may use and what it never shares. Customer personal details and records covered by privacy rules stay out unless your own policy allows them, and a person reviews anything before it goes out.
