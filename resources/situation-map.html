Situation Map

One page to make the right step easier to take, for the one task that keeps slipping however committed everyone is.

Inside the map

  • Name the task that keeps slipping and what usually gets in the way
  • Space: what could change about when, where, or with what tools the task happens
  • Peers: who would raise your standard on this
  • Mentors: one specific question for someone a few steps ahead
  • Culture: one shared norm that would make this the obvious thing to do
  • One change to test for two weeks, plus a pocket card to keep where the task happens

About fifteen minutes. The four rings come from Angela Duckworth’s Situated, and the field note Situational Agency: Duckworth’s Situated for Small Teams explains them.

Two fields and you are done. The third is optional.
