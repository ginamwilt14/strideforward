Situation, what we built, what changed. Organizations are described rather than named unless they have said yes to being named.

An early-learning media startup

Building the evidence base and partnership plan for a new product.

Situation
A founder with a product for four- to six-year-olds and strong instincts, but no structured evidence or partnership approach to bring to schools and families.
What we built
A research brief connecting the product to learning science, a pilot plan across parents, homeschool, and classroom settings, and a partnership development approach.
What changed
Product decisions now have a Now, Next, Later logic tied to evidence, and partner conversations start from a clear story.

HIKI Education · Hawaiʻi

Positioning place-based professional learning for schools.

Situation
Strong, culturally grounded programs and a fishpond site, but no clear way for principals and district leaders to understand or contract the professional learning.
What we built
A school-buyer strategy, a soft-launch website, professional-learning packages, a scoping-call-to-proposal path, an operating playbook, and pitch materials for funders and partners.
What changed
School leaders can now see what is offered, who it is for, and how to begin. The team has a playbook to run it.

Who you met

Gina Wilt, EdD, MBA.

Gina founded StrideForward to give small teams a thinking partner and a working partner. She is based in Phoenix, Arizona, and works with clients in person and remotely.

  • More than twenty years building learning programs and education ventures, from the special education classroom and instructional coaching to teacher-preparation faculty.
  • Led curriculum as an associate vice president for an online university.
  • Directed a market-facing professional learning business inside a large public university, building about $585,000 in partnerships and sales with school systems and national education organizations.
  • Nine months as business architect for HIKI Education in Hawaiʻi: school-buyer strategy, a website, packages, a proposal path, an operating playbook, and workflow systems.
  • Three months shaping a dual-enrollment strategy (college credit for high school students) for an arts-and-humanities education organization.
  • Currently the fractional partnerships and business development manager for an early-learning company.
  • Runs her own small businesses on the same systems she sets up for clients: booking, email follow-up, and CRM.
