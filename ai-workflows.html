Human-centered by design

Built around the people
who do the work.

A workflow only helps if people trust it and use it. Every setup follows the same three steps.

  1. 01

    Start with the people and the work.

    We look at where your team’s time goes, ask what they would gladly hand off and what they want to keep, and pick two or three workflows to test first.

  2. 02

    People make the calls.

    AI drafts, sorts, and reminds. A person reviews anything that reaches a customer, partner, or funder, and the team decides what AI takes on next.

  3. 03

    Hand it over working.

    Set up in your accounts, documented in plain language, and your team trained to run and change it. Fractional support can keep it current as the tools change.

Try this first

Find your first workflow
in twenty minutes.

You can do this on your own before a call. Bring your list, and we will start there.

  1. List the tasks your team repeats every week. Emails you write again and again, reports you rebuild, notes you turn into to-dos.

  2. Mark the ones that follow the same steps each time. Those are the easiest to hand to AI.

  3. Circle one where a mistake is easy to catch. A draft someone reviews before it goes out is a safe place to start.

  4. Write down what a good result looks like. One example of your best version gives the AI something to match.

  5. Try it with one person for two weeks. Keep what saves time, fix what does not, then add the next one.

Not sure where to start? Look at follow-up.

  • New leads wait more than a day for a first reply.
  • Each follow-up email is written from scratch.
  • Only one person knows how to answer the common questions.
  • Your CRM is out of date by Friday.
  • You prepare for calls by scrolling through old emails.

If two or more of those sound familiar, sales follow-up is a good first workflow.

Common questions

Questions about AI
and your team.

Short answers about tools, people, and rules. If yours is not here, ask on the call.

Do we need to be technical to use AI workflows?

No. We set the workflows up in the tools you already use and write the instructions in plain language. Training covers how to run them, how to check the results, and how to change them when your work changes.

Which AI tools do you work with?

We start with what your team already has. Gina’s own workflows run on Claude, connected to email, calendar, documents, and task boards. If your team uses another AI assistant, we look at what it can do on the first call.

Will AI replace people on my team?

We design workflows to take repeat work off people’s plates, so they have more time for customers, partners, and the work that needs a person. Your team decides which tasks AI takes on, and a person reviews anything that matters.

How do you handle privacy and accuracy?

Each workflow has rules written in: what information it may use, what it must never send or share, and which steps need a person to check. We can also write a short AI use policy with your team, so everyone works from the same rules.

Can you train my team to use AI?

Yes. Training is part of every setup: short, hands-on sessions where people practice with their own work, plus simple guidelines they can keep. If your team already has the tools set up, training can be its own project.
