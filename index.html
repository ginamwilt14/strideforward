Work like yours

Three snapshots
of the work.

Situation, what we built, what changed. Organizations are described rather than named unless they have said yes to being named.

A college of education at a large public university

Making a workforce portfolio legible to outside buyers.

Situation
Learning, credentials, consulting, research, and events had grown up separately. Districts, employers, and partners could not see how the pieces fit or where to start.
What we built
A connected offering and capability strategy: audience-specific offers, pathways between them, and the market intelligence and financial logic behind each.
What changed
One portfolio that external partners could read, with clear entry points and a shared language across teams.

HIKI Education · Hawaiʻi

Positioning place-based professional learning for schools.

Situation
Strong, culturally grounded programs and a fishpond site, but no clear way for principals and district leaders to understand or contract the professional learning.
What we built
A school-buyer strategy, a soft-launch website, professional-learning packages, a scoping-call-to-proposal path, an operating playbook, and pitch materials for funders and partners.
What changed
School leaders can now see what is offered, who it is for, and how to begin. The team has a playbook to run it.

An early-learning media startup

Building the evidence base and partnership plan for a new product.

Situation
A founder with a product for four- to six-year-olds and strong instincts, but no structured evidence or partnership approach to bring to schools and families.
What we built
A research brief connecting the product to learning science, a pilot plan across parents, homeschool, and classroom settings, and a partnership development approach.
What changed
Product decisions now have a Now, Next, Later logic tied to evidence, and partner conversations start from a clear story.

Common questions

Questions people ask before they reach out.

Short answers about who we work with and how the work starts. If yours is not here, ask.

Who does StrideForward work with?

Entrepreneurs, small business owners, and leaders of small nonprofits and community organizations, plus schools and learning ventures that need program design. Most clients have a small team, or no team, and need work done rather than a report.

What kind of work do you take on?

Six kinds: market research and positioning, program development, new revenue services and products, customer experience design, business development and sales, and marketing and sales operations. We work as a defined project or as a fractional partner inside your team.

What does fractional mean?

A fractional partner works inside your team for set hours each month on agreed priorities, for a monthly fee. You get senior help with growth, programs, or operations before you are ready to hire for it, and we review the scope every quarter.

Do you advise, or do you do the work?

Both. We think the problem through with you, then build the pieces: research, program structures, materials, pilots, outreach, and the systems behind them.

What happens on a discovery call?

Thirty minutes on video. You describe what you are trying to make possible and what is in the way; we work out together which kind of help fits, if any. You leave with one clear next step. If it is a fit, Gina sends a one-page scope note within two business days.

How does a project start, and how are fees set?

With a conversation about your goals and constraints. We then propose scope, timeline, responsibilities, and a fee, so you know what is included before you commit.

How is StrideForward different from NextReady Studio and Thrive Vibes?

All three are GMW Group brands. StrideForward does consulting and design work for your organization. NextReady Studio teaches practical skills through workshops and coaching. Thrive Vibes offers entrepreneurial experiences, community, and learning travel.

Can you train my team on everyday work skills?

For professional learning built around your team’s goals and practice, yes. That starts with a discovery call. For ready-made practical skills workshops, NextReady Studio, a sister brand, runs scheduled public dates and private sessions for small teams.

Where are you based?

Phoenix, Arizona. We work with clients in person and remotely.
