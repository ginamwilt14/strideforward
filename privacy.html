How information submitted through this site is handled.

Who operates this site

StrideForward is a brand of GMW Group LLC. For questions about your information, contact hello@strideforward.world.

Inquiries and resource requests

Forms collect the information you enter, such as your name, email, organization, and message. We use it to respond to your inquiry or handle your resource request. Resource requests do not automatically subscribe you to a newsletter.

Services used by the site

Netlify hosts the website and processes website form submissions. The K–12 newsletter signup uses MailerLite. These services receive information needed to provide their functions. Website fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives the technical request from your browser.

Newsletter choices

Newsletter signup is separate from general inquiries and downloads. Use the unsubscribe option in any newsletter email to stop receiving it. Contact us if you need help.

Browser preferences

The site starts with your system’s light or dark preference. The theme button changes the current page without storing a persistent preference. The supplied website code does not include an advertising pixel or an analytics tracking script. Hosting services and newsletter tools may process technical information for operation and security.

Your requests

Email us to ask about, correct, or request deletion of information you have submitted. Please do not send sensitive personal information through the website forms.
